Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Casino Chinese ("the App") is a vocational Mandarin-language learning app for casino and gaming-industry staff. This page explains what information the App accesses, how it's used, and — just as important — what we don't do with it.

The short version

  • The App does not require an account, and does not collect or transmit any personal data to our servers.
  • Your lesson progress and quiz scores are stored only on your device, and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The microphone is used only when you actively tap the microphone button during Speaking practice, and only to check your pronunciation in that moment.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

The Speaking practice feature lets you record yourself saying a phrase and see how closely it matches. To do this, the App uses your device's built-in operating system speech-recognition service (Apple's Speech framework on iOS, or the equivalent Android system service). Depending on your device, iOS/Android version, and settings, this recognition may happen entirely on your device, or your device's operating system may send the audio to Apple's or Google's servers for processing — that determination and any related data handling is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policies, not ours.

We do not receive, store, or have any access to your voice recordings. The App only receives the final recognized text back from the operating system, compares it locally on your device to the target phrase, and discards it. Nothing related to your voice is saved or transmitted to us.

You can decline microphone permission entirely — Speaking practice will still work in a "listen and mark as practiced" mode without it.

What we store, and where

The App tracks which lessons and activities you've completed, and your quiz history, so it can show you progress bars and let you review words you've missed. This is stored using your device's local app storage (technically, AsyncStorage) and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this data. We have no server, so we have no copy of it either.

Third parties

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). The only external services involved are your device's own operating-system text-to-speech and speech-recognition capabilities, described above.

Children's privacy

Casino Chinese is a professional/vocational training tool intended for adult gaming-industry staff and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the App collects no personal data on any server in the first place, this is true for users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Since the App doesn't collect contact information from you, we won't be able to notify you directly — please check back here periodically.
